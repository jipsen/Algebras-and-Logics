# Commutative integral ordered monoids

Abbreviation: **CIOMon**

## Definition
A ***commutative integral ordered monoid*** is a [commutative (totally) ordered monoid](commutative_(totally)_ordered_monoids.md) $\mathbf{A}=\langle A,\cdot,1,\le\rangle$ that is

***integral***:  $x\le 1$

### Morphisms
Let $\mathbf{A}$ and $\mathbf{B}$ be commutative integral ordered monoids. A morphism from $\mathbf{A}$ to $\mathbf{B}$ is a function $h:A\rightarrow B$ that is a orderpreserving homomorphism: 
$h(x \cdot y)=h(x) \cdot h(y)$, $h(1)=1$, and $x\le y\Longrightarrow h(x)\le h(y)$.
