# Quasitrivial groupoids

Abbreviation: **QtGrpd**

## Definition
A ***quasitrivial groupoid*** is a [groupoid](groupoids.md) $\mathbf{A}=\langle A,\cdot\rangle$ such that

$\cdot$ is ***quasitrivial***:  $x\cdot y=x\text{ or }x\cdot y=y$

### Morphisms
Let $\mathbf{A}$ and $\mathbf{B}$ be quasitrivial groupoids. A morphism from $\mathbf{A}$ to $\mathbf{B}$ is a function $h:A\rightarrow B$ that is a homomorphism: 
$h(x \cdot y)=h(x) \cdot h(y)$
